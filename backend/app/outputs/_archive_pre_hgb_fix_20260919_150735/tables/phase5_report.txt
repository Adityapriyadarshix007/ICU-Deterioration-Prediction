----------------------------------------------------------------------
LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. MAR vs MNAR cannot be distinguished statistically.
     Any method claiming to do so makes untestable
     assumptions. We assume MAR for imputation purposes
     and acknowledge this as a limitation.

  2. If MCAR was rejected, we know only that missingness
     is not completely random. We do not know whether it
     is MAR or MNAR.

  3. Little's MCAR test is hypersensitive at large N.
     Rejection indicates non-exact-MCAR, not strong
     evidence against MCAR.

  4. Chi-square p-values are inflated by sample size.
     Cramér's V is reported as the primary effect size
     and should be used to prioritize features.
